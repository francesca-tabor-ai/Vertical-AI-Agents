# Phase 1: Niche Selection - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Objective
Identify and validate a high-value niche for your vertical AI agent business in 2-4 weeks.

---

## 📋 WEEK-BY-WEEK PLAN

### Week 1: Problem Discovery & Research

**Monday-Tuesday: Brainstorm Potential Niches**
- [ ] Complete `01-Problem-Discovery-Checklist.md`
- [ ] List 5-10 potential problems to solve
- [ ] Score each on 6 key criteria
- [ ] Select top 3 for deeper research

**Wednesday-Friday: Deep Industry Research**
- [ ] Complete `02-Industry-Research-Template.md` for each niche
- [ ] Research market size, buyer personas, competitors
- [ ] Analyze data availability and integration options
- [ ] Document findings

**Weekend: Financial Modeling**
- [ ] Work through `04-Financial-Viability-Calculator.md`
- [ ] Model revenue, costs, and profitability
- [ ] Ensure LTV:CAC ratio > 5:1
- [ ] Confirm break-even < 3 months

---

### Week 2: Niche Scoring & Selection

**Monday-Wednesday: Score & Compare**
- [ ] Complete `05-Niche-Scoring-Matrix.md`
- [ ] Score all 3 niches across 7 dimensions
- [ ] Calculate weighted totals
- [ ] Identify top-scoring niche

**Thursday: Selection Decision**
- [ ] Choose your niche (score 7.0+ required)
- [ ] Document your unfair advantage
- [ ] Set 90-day revenue goal
- [ ] Create project folder

**Friday: Prepare for Validation**
- [ ] Read `03-Customer-Validation-Script.md` thoroughly
- [ ] Create list of 20 potential interviewees
- [ ] Draft outreach messages
- [ ] Set up calendar for booking calls

---

### Week 3-4: Customer Validation

**Goal: Complete 10 discovery calls**

**Daily Tasks:**
- [ ] Send 5 outreach messages (LinkedIn/email)
- [ ] Conduct 2-3 discovery calls
- [ ] Document findings immediately after each call
- [ ] Update validation tracker

**What You're Validating:**
1. Problem severity (target: 8+/10)
2. Willingness to pay (target: $500+/month)
3. Pilot commitment (target: 3-5 customers)
4. Data accessibility (must be feasible)

**End of Week 4 Decision:**
- ✅ **GREEN LIGHT:** 60%+ rated pain 8+, 3+ pilot commits → Build it
- ⚠️ **YELLOW LIGHT:** Mixed results → Refine and do 5 more calls
- 🛑 **RED LIGHT:** Low interest → Choose different niche

## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### 1. Skipping Customer Validation
**Mistake:** "I know this is a problem, I'll just build it"
**Why it fails:** Your assumptions are often wrong
**Fix:** Force yourself to do 10 calls before building

### 2. Choosing Too Broad a Niche
**Mistake:** "I'll help all manufacturing companies"
**Why it fails:** Too generic, can't compete
**Fix:** Niche down → "Inventory reorder for food manufacturers"

### 3. Ignoring Data Accessibility
**Mistake:** "They must have this data somewhere"
**Why it fails:** Integration nightmares kill momentum
**Fix:** Validate data access in first 3 customer calls

### 4. Complex Delivery Mechanisms
**Mistake:** "I'll build a custom dashboard"
**Why it fails:** Takes forever, users won't adopt
**Fix:** Use email/Slack until customers demand more

### 5. Underpricing
**Mistake:** "I'll charge $50/month to get started"
**Why it fails:** Not sustainable, attracts wrong customers
**Fix:** Charge for value, not time ($500+ minimum)

### 6. Building for Free Users
**Mistake:** "I'll let them try it free for 3 months"
**Why it fails:** They'll never convert
**Fix:** Paid pilots only, even if discounted

### 7. Picking Boring Niches
**Mistake:** Choosing highest-scoring niche you hate
**Why it fails:** You'll burn out in 6 months
**Fix:** Need 7.0+ score AND personal excitement

## 📊 VALIDATION RED FLAGS

**Stop and reconsider if you hear:**

🚩 "This sounds interesting" (not a commitment)
🚩 "Can we try it for free first?" (won't convert)
🚩 "I'd need to get approval from [many people]" (long sales cycle)
🚩 "We're already using [expensive tool]" (unlikely to switch)
🚩 "This would be nice to have" (not urgent)
🚩 "Can you customize it for our specific needs?" (time sink)

**Green flags to pursue:**

✅ "We're currently paying $X for this" (budget exists)
✅ "This takes us Y hours per week" (quantified pain)
✅ "When can we start?" (urgency)
✅ "How much does it cost?" (buying mindset)
✅ "Can I introduce you to [decision maker]?" (champion)
✅ "We've tried 3 other solutions" (active buyer)
