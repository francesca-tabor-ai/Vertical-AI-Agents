## 💡 Pro Tips for GEO Experts

**Leverage your existing knowledge:**
- Industries you've already helped with GEO need vertical agents too
- Your understanding of AI platforms = you know what's possible
- Use your network for warm introductions

**Red flags from research:**
- Can't find 10+ forum posts about the problem
- No existing (even bad) solutions in market
- Data requires complex enterprise integrations
- Buyer persona has no budget authority

**Green flags from research:**
- People complaining about problem in last 30 days
- Existing solutions cost $10K+/year
- Clear API access to needed data
- Title with "Manager" or above = budget authority
