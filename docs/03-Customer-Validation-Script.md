# Customer Validation Script

## 🗣️ DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT (15-20 Minutes)

### OPENING (2 minutes)

**You:**
"Thanks for taking the time. I'm researching [problem space] and talking to 
folks like you to understand how it's currently handled. 

Before we start - is 15-20 minutes still okay?"

**Them:** [Confirm]

**You:**
"Great. Just to set expectations - I'm in research mode, not selling anything 
today. I'm genuinely trying to understand if [problem] is worth solving and 
how people currently deal with it."

**Why this works:** Removes sales pressure, increases honesty

---

### PART 1: Current State Discovery (5-7 minutes)

**Frame:**
"Let me start by understanding your current process..."

**Question 1: Workflow**
"Walk me through what you do when it's time to [specific task]. 

What's the step-by-step process?"

**Listen for:**
- Manual steps
- Tools/software used
- Data sources
- Time spent
- Dependencies on others

**Probing questions:**
- "How often does this happen?"
- "Who else is involved?"
- "What tools do you use for this?"

---

**Question 2: Pain Points**
"What's most frustrating about the current approach?"

**Listen for:**
- Time consumption
- Error-proneness
- Complexity
- Lack of tools
- Manual/repetitive nature

**Probing questions:**
- "How much time does this take per week/month?"
- "What happens when mistakes are made?"
- "Have you tried other solutions?"

---

**Question 3: Quantify the Pain**
"If you had to estimate - how many hours per week/month do you spend on this?"

**Listen for:**
- Specific numbers (not "a lot")
- Seasonal variations
- Who's involved beyond them

**Probing questions:**
- "Is that just you, or does your team help?"
- "What could you be doing instead with that time?"
- "What does an error cost the business?"

---

### PART 2: Attempted Solutions (3-4 minutes)

**Question 4: What Have They Tried?**
"What have you tried to make this faster or easier?"

**Listen for:**
- Existing software (competitors)
- Homegrown solutions
- Workarounds
- Why previous solutions failed

**Probing questions:**
- "What did you like/dislike about [tool they mentioned]?"
- "Why did you stop using it?"
- "What would the perfect solution look like?"

---

**Question 5: Decision Process**
"If you found a solution that worked, how would you go about buying it?"

**Listen for:**
- Budget authority
- Approval process
- Procurement requirements
- Buying timeline

**Probing questions:**
- "Do you have budget for tools like this?"
- "Who else would need to approve?"
- "When do budget decisions typically happen?"

---

### PART 3: Solution Validation (5-7 minutes)

**Transition:**
"This is really helpful. Let me share what I'm working on and get your reaction..."

**Present Concept:**
"I'm exploring an automated service that [brief description of what agent does].

It would work like this:
1. [Integration step] - e.g., connects to your data via email/API
2. [Analysis step] - e.g., runs analysis weekly
3. [Delivery step] - e.g., sends recommendations via Slack
4. [Action step] - e.g., you approve/reject with one click

The goal is to reduce [specific task] from [X hours] to [Y minutes]."

---

**Question 6: Reaction**
"What's your initial reaction to something like that?"

**Listen for:**
- Excitement vs. skepticism
- Specific concerns
- Feature requests
- Comparison to existing tools

**Probing questions:**
- "What would make you trust the recommendations?"
- "What would prevent you from using this?"
- "What's missing from what I described?"

---

**Question 7: The Magic Question**
"If this worked reliably, what would you be willing to pay per month?"

**Listen for:**
- Specific number
- Comparison to current costs
- Budget constraints
- Hesitation

**If they hesitate:**
"Let me frame it differently - you mentioned spending [X hours/week] on this. 
If you could get that time back for $[reasonable price]/month, would that 
be worth it?"

---

**Question 8: The Commitment Test**
"If I build this in the next 4-6 weeks, would you be interested in being 
one of the first users?

It would be a pilot - $[50% of target price] per month, and you'd help 
me refine it with feedback."

**Listen for:**
- Immediate "yes" = strong signal
- "Maybe" = lukewarm, dig deeper
- "Need to think about it" = probably not ready
- "Yes but need approval" = understand approval process

**If YES:**
"Fantastic. What would need to happen for you to say 'this is working' 
after 30 days?"

**If MAYBE:**
"What would need to change for this to be a 'yes' for you?"

---

### CLOSING (2 minutes)

**You:**
"This has been incredibly helpful. A few final questions:

1. Is there anyone else in [Industry] dealing with this that I should talk to?
2. Would you be open to a follow-up call once I have a prototype?
3. Can I email you a summary of what I'm building?"

**Get:**
- [ ] Referrals (2-3 names if possible)
- [ ] Permission to follow up
- [ ] Email address

**Thank them:**
"Really appreciate your time and honesty. I'll keep you posted on progress."

### DECISION CRITERIA

**GREEN LIGHT (Build it):**
- ✅ 60%+ rated pain as 8-10
- ✅ 40%+ would pay $500+/month
- ✅ 3+ committed to pilot
- ✅ Clear patterns in what they want

**YELLOW LIGHT (Refine):**
- ⚠️ 40-60% rated pain as 8-10
- ⚠️ 20-40% would pay $500+/month
- ⚠️ 1-2 pilot commitments
- ⚠️ Mixed feedback on solution

**RED LIGHT (Pivot):**
- 🛑 <40% rated pain as 8-10
- 🛑 <20% would pay meaningful amount
- 🛑 0 pilot commitments
- 🛑 Fundamental objections to approach

## 💰 PRICING VALIDATION

### Price Anchoring Technique

**During Call, Try This:**
"Just to calibrate - would $2,000/month be reasonable for solving this?"

**If they say:**
- "Yes" → You can charge more, try $3K
- "That's high" → Try $1,000 range
- "Way too much" → Understand their budget ceiling

**Goal:** Find the price where 60% say "yes" without hesitation

---

### Value-Based Pricing Formula

**Customer's current cost:**
- Hours/month: ____ × Hourly rate: $____ = $____/month
- Error cost: $____/month
- Opportunity cost: $____/month
- **TOTAL:** $____/month

**Your price target:** 20-30% of total cost

**Example:**
- Customer spends: $5,000/month (labor + errors)
- Your price: $1,000-1,500/month
- Their savings: $3,500-4,000/month
- ROI: 3-4x

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Opening the conversation:**
- ❌ "Would you buy my solution?"
- ✅ "Tell me about how you currently handle [X]"

**Handling objections:**
- ❌ Argue or oversell
- ✅ "That's interesting - tell me more about that concern"

**Getting real data:**
- ❌ "Would this be useful?"
- ✅ "How many hours per week do you spend on this?"

**Testing commitment:**
- ❌ "Would you maybe be interested someday?"
- ✅ "If I build this in 6 weeks, will you pilot it for $X?"

**Leveraging your GEO background:**
- Mention you help companies optimize for AI discovery
- Position as someone who understands AI's capabilities
- Offer to share AI adoption insights from other industries
